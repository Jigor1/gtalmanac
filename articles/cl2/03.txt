GTA 6 gameplay leak footage has surfaced again online, with the leaker Cyberleek releasing about 25 minutes of video on October 8, 2026. The clip reportedly includes cutscenes, mission gameplay and story spoilers from Rockstar Games’ upcoming title. The footage has not been independently verified, and Rockstar has not commented on this upload yet. The leak arrives 6 weeks before the GTA 6 release date of November 19. 
Here is what the footage shows and how Rockstar and Take-Two have responded so far. 
GTA 6 Gameplay Leak: What We Know So Far 
Cyberleek posted the footage on October 8. It reportedly comes as a ZIP file larger than 370MB, and the leaker named it “Extended Leek.” The video runs for nearly 25 minutes. 
- Advertisement - 
According to reports, it includes cutscenes, missions, and story spoilers. Jason, one of the game’s 2 protagonists, appears nude in much of it. His nudity was already a topic online earlier this week. 

Beware, Cyberleek has released 367 MB worth of #GTAVI leaked footage. — Tex2 (@TexFunz2) October 8, 2026 
It is not clear how Cyberleek obtained the footage. The GTA 6 leaked footage looks recent. It may come from the full interview and preview that media released on October 7, since the leak shows almost everything covered there. 
It remains unclear whether the person or group behind the August Cyberleek GTA 6 leak uploaded this one. Rockstar has not publicly addressed the video at the time of writing. 
Scenes Shown in the GTA 6 Leaked Footage 
An early rundown of the GTA 6 gameplay leak, which it still lists as a developing story, describes these scenes: 
A cutscene shows Jason and Lucia visiting a trailer store and changing clothes. 
A scripted mission follows, with a dirt bike race. 
Cyberleek cuts in a promotion for his crypto coin. 
Jason rides a motorbike, walks across a bridge, and shoots a tank that appears above a waterfall. 
A spoof car advert plays. 
Cutscenes show Jason talking to other characters at a beachside coffee shop and about car racing. 
Jason tunes a car engine, then drives in first person using nitrous. 
An X user described more of the video. The user mentioned racing in Vice City, a location called the Thrillbilly Mud Club, and vehicle modification. The same user said Lucia appears in a bikini. 
The video contains story spoilers. Readers who want to avoid them should be careful on social media today.

Who Is Cyberleek? 
Cyberleek first appeared in August 2026. The leaks began on August 18 with videos of Jason playing basketball and driving. More clips followed over the next days, including the nine1nine nightclub and a police chase through Vice City . On August 24, footage of a nudist community showed Jason watching NPCs through binoculars. 
The leaker says the releases are a protest against digital-only games, preorders, and monetization practices. Cyberleek also promoted a cryptocurrency next to the footage, which raises questions about the motive. 
Cyberleek then stayed quiet for weeks. Activity picked up again on October 5, when visitors spotted a poll with question marks on the leaker’s website. Voting was due to end on October 8. 
Rockstar Games Statement and Take-Two Legal Action 
The Rockstar Games statement came on August 26, after the first wave of leaks. Rockstar called the leak “heartbreaking” for its team and said this was not how it wanted players to see the game. It also said the game is nearly finished and thanked fans for their support. 
Take-Two, the owner of Rockstar, took legal action . Reports say it issued subpoenas to platforms including Microsoft, Discord and X to trace the source of the leaks. Court filings describe the investigation as fast-moving.
