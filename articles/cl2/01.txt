GTA 6 leaker Cyberleek has returned to the internet after weeks of silence, releasing a massive new batch of alleged gameplay footage. 

Cyberleek first made headlines in August 2026, when they began releasing footage of Rockstar’s highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 . The leaks offered an early look at everything from combat and driving mechanics to NPC interactions, radio stations, and previously unseen locations. 
The leaks eventually prompted a response from Rockstar Games , which described the situation as “heartbreaking,” while publisher Take-Two began taking legal action to identify the person responsible. 
Now, with GTA 6’s November 19 release date approaching, Cyberleek returned with another substantial leak. 
Cyberleek releases massive new GTA 6 gameplay leak 
On October 8, a new leak attributed to Cyberleek surfaced online, reportedly consisting of a ZIP file larger than 370MB containing approximately 25 minutes of gameplay footage. He titled the file “Extended Leek”. 
The footage shows Jason running around the game world completely nude , and Lucia similarly exposed, wearing only bikini bottoms.

This also isn’t the first time nudity has featured in Cyberleek’s leaks. On August 24, the leaker released footage of an apparent nudist community, showing Jason using binoculars to observe NPCs without clothing. 
Who is Cyberleek? 
Cyberleek’s original leaks began on August 18, with videos showing Jason playing basketball, driving, and fighting NPCs. More footage followed over the next several days, including a look inside the nine1nine nightclub and a police chase through Vice City. 
The anonymous leaker has claimed the releases are a protest against digital-only games, preorders, and monetization practices. However, Cyberleek also promoted a cryptocurrency alongside the footage, raising questions about their motivations .

Take-Two subsequently issued subpoenas to several platforms as part of its efforts to identify the source of the leaks, with court filings describing the investigation as “rapidly evolving.” 
It remains unclear whether the latest upload comes from the same individual or group responsible for August’s leaks, and the authenticity of the new footage has not been independently verified. 
Rockstar has not publicly addressed the October 8 footage at the time of writing.
