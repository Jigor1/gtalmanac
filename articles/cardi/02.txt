Cardi B has dropped hew new song “Don’t Chart” from the upcoming official soundtrack, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album . 
The full album, featuring 34 original tracks, is not dropping until November 19, but Atlantic Records has been dropping songs slowly leading up to the release.

There are now eight songs from the soundtrack out in the world, including Morgan Wallen’ s “Last Thing You Need” and Travis Scott ‘s “RHYNO.”
