Atlantic Records has released a new single by Cardi B titled “Don’t Chart,” part of the upcoming official soundtrack for Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album . The track arrives as part of a phased rollout leading up to the full album’s debut. 
Song release details and lyrics 
The complete soundtrack, which contains 34 original tracks, is scheduled for release on November 19. In the interim, Atlantic Records has been issuing individual songs from the collection. Cardi B announced the drop just hours before its availability, posting the opening phrase “B***h be still” on social media. 
In the chorus, the artist states, “I’m still pretty, still bad, I’m still thick / I’m still me, therefore still talkin’ my sh**, look / Suckin’ the gut, uh, f*** out the butt, look / I got the GoPro on holdin’ ’em up / I’m still poppin’, still winnin’, you still hatin’ / Heard you gon’ do somethin’ to me, I’m still waitin’.” 

Other tracks on the soundtrack 
With this release, eight songs from the Grand Theft Auto VI soundtrack are now publicly available. Previous drops include Morgan Wallen’s “Last Thing You Need” and Travis Scott’s “RHYNO.”
