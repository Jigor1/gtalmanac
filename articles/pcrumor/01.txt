The PC release window for Grand Theft Auto 6 is one of the biggest questions in the gaming world today, since Rockstar Games confirmed its exclusive release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series S/X on November 19, 2026 , which isn't surprising, given that's what they've always done. Now an insider is claiming to have a specific date, and if accurate, it would be significantly earlier than what Rockstar's historical pattern suggests. 
DetectiveSeeds, a leaker who has previously shared information about Rockstar's plans, posted on X that according to three former Rockstar employees he contacted through LinkedIn, Rockstar is "aiming to release the PC version in February 2027." However, he added that all three sources stated that this isn't final, but that Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar would prefer to get the PC version out before the fiscal year ends in March. 
According to the known Rockstar insider, he reached out to over 90 Rockstar employees on LinkedIn, and only three answered him. 

As per our PC release guide , the industry consensus right now is Q4 2027, which means October to December 2027. This is an educated estimate based on Rockstar's historical pattern. Grand Theft Auto V had a roughly 19-month gap between its console launch in September 2013 and its PC release in April 2015. Red Dead Redemption 2 had a 13-month gap between its console launch in October 2018 and its PC release in November 2019. Applying a 12-to-18-month range to a November 2026 console launch puts the expected PC window between November 2027 and May 2028.

February 2027 is dramatically shorter than anything Rockstar has done before, but the financial incentive makes sense. Take-Two's fiscal year ends on March 31. A February 2027 PC release would allow the company to book PC revenue within the same fiscal year as the console launch, which runs from April 2026 to March 2027. From a financial reporting perspective, this would make the game's first-year revenue figures look substantially larger in a single reporting period, which matters enormously for investor presentations and stock price. DFC Intelligence projects GTA 6 will generate $3.2 billion in its first year . Adding PC revenue to that same fiscal year would push the number even higher.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has acknowledged that PC "is becoming a much greater share of so-called console releases." The PC gaming market has grown substantially since RDR2 launched in 2019 and Steam's monthly active user base has increased as the audience for AAA PC releases has expanded. The financial incentive to release on PC sooner rather than later is stronger than ever. 
However, the reasons for Rockstar's delays in PC releases remain. Console-exclusive launch windows drive hardware sales and earn Rockstar favorable marketing treatment from Sony and Microsoft, both of whom have been formally told the November 19 date is firm . A simultaneous or near-simultaneous PC release would undermine whatever exclusivity agreements or marketing partnerships exist with the console platforms. The piracy risk on PC, while reduced by modern DRM, is also a factor.

Three months is not enough time to do any of those things effectively. It is barely enough time for the console launch hype cycle to conclude before the PC announcement begins generating its own attention. If Rockstar is genuinely targeting February 2027, it would suggest either that the console exclusivity window is not like what it used to be, or the financial pressure to consolidate revenue within a single fiscal year is overriding the traditional marketing strategy, or that the PC version is further along in development than anyone outside the studio realized. 
Ultimately, the practical advice for PC gamers remains the same: do not buy hardware yet. If the PC version arrives in February 2027, that is still ten months away. If it arrives in Q4 2027, which remains the more conservative and historically supported estimate, that is 18 months away. Either way, GPU and RAM prices are in flux due to the DRAM shortage , and next-generation hardware will offer better value by the time the PC version actually ships, regardless of when that turns out to be. 
Just to be clear, Rockstar has not confirmed a PC version of GTA 6 . The company has not announced a PC release date. Everything in this article is based on an unverified claim from a leaker citing former employees.
